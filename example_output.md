# 🌸 Your Mood-Based Recipe

## Dish: Citrus Blossom Risotto  
**Subtitle:** A silky bowl of longing, kissed by yuzu and shiso

---

## 🍽️ Ingredients

- Arborio rice – 100g  
- Yuzu zest – 1 tsp  
- Shiso oil – 1 tbsp  
- Vegetable broth – 500ml  
- Coconut cream – 2 tbsp  
- Salt – to taste

---

## 🔥 Cooking Directions

1. Sauté rice in shiso oil until fragrant  
2. Gradually add broth, stirring until creamy  
3. Fold in coconut cream and yuzu zest  
4. Plate gently and drizzle with extra shiso oil
